Privacy policy: Arkusch website

Last updated: October 2026

This page explains how this website (the Arkusch start page) handles data. The app has its own privacy policy: App privacy policy

Who is responsible

The person named in the imprint is responsible for this website: Imprint

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc. (USA). When you open a page, GitHub's servers process technical connection data such as your IP address, date and time, and the requested file, so that the page can be delivered and the service kept secure. I have no access to these logs. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure and reliable website). More in GitHub's privacy statement: GitHub privacy statement

No cookies, no tracking

This website uses no cookies, no analytics or advertising services and no social-media plug-ins. Fonts and images are served from this site itself, so no data is sent to Google Fonts or other third parties.

Your settings in the browser

If you choose a language or the Outdoor/Indoor appearance, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (keys arkusch-lang and arkusch-theme) so the page remembers it. This data stays on your device, is not sent to a server, and can be deleted in your browser settings. It is used only to provide the function you asked for (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

Videos

Short clips on this page, if any, are delivered by this website itself and load nothing from third parties.

Links and contact

Links to the App Store, to my personal website and to other external pages lead to services with their own privacy policies; once you follow a link, those apply. If you write to me by e-mail or through my website, I use your details only to reply to you.

Your rights
