# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: July 2026*

### Offline by design

Your specimen data are your own. This application is built with a local-first philosophy.

**Local Storage & Synchronization**
All data—including scientific names, location logs, lineage notes, and photographic records—is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. 

If you enable iCloud synchronization, your data is securely transferred and stored in your **private iCloud account** using Apple's CloudKit framework. This allows you to sync your data across your own devices. This data is encrypted end-to-end; Apple cannot access it, and neither can I. I have no access to your personal iCloud data.

**No Accounts**
There is no registration or login required. I do not collect or store any personal identity information. Your usage remains entirely anonymous.

**System Diagnostics**
If the app crashes, anonymous technical logs are sent via Firebase Crashlytics to help improve stability. Firebase Crashlytics is operated by Google. It automatically deletes crash data after 90 days. You can learn more about Google's data practices here: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Data Protection**
This app complies with GDPR and applicable data protection laws. No user-identifiable data is transmitted. The app does not track your usage patterns or monitor your behavior.

### Permissions

**Camera & Photo Library**
The camera and photo library are accessed solely to allow you to attach imagery to your plant records. Images are stored locally on your device (and synced via your private iCloud if enabled) and are never shared or transmitted.

**Location Services**
The app utilizes GPS coordinates only when you explicitly trigger the "Get GPS Pin" function while logging an outdoor specimen. This data is stored locally on your device for your reference and is not shared with any third party.

### Your control

You have total control over your data:
*   **Delete Entries:** You can delete individual specimen logs at any time within the app.
*   **Wipe Data:** Deleting the app or clearing its data via your device settings will permanently remove all stored specimens and imagery. If iCloud sync is enabled, data is removed from your devices according to Apple's synchronization protocols.
*   **Data Portability:** You can export your entire specimen database at any time as a **CSV file**. This ensures you can easily open your records in spreadsheet software (like Excel or Numbers) or move them to other applications whenever you choose.
