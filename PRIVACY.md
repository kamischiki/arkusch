# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 2026*

### Offline by design

Your specimen data are your own. This application is built with a local-first philosophy.

**Local Storage & Synchronization** All data—including scientific names, location logs, lineage notes, and photographic records—is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

If you enable iCloud synchronization, your data is also stored in your **private iCloud account** using Apple's CloudKit framework, which lets you sync across your own devices. It is protected by Apple's iCloud security measures, and Apple's handling of it is governed by Apple's own privacy policy. As the developer, I have no access to your iCloud data.

**No Accounts** There is no registration or login required. I do not collect or store any information about your identity, such as your name or email address. I don't know who you are.

**System Diagnostics** If the app crashes, technical crash logs are sent via Firebase Crashlytics to help improve stability. Firebase Crashlytics is operated by Google. It automatically deletes crash data after 90 days. You can learn more about Google's data practices here: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Data Protection** Apart from the crash diagnostics described above, I do not collect any data about you. Your data leaves your device only if you enable iCloud synchronization, in which case it is stored in your private iCloud account. The app does not track your usage patterns or monitor your behavior.

### Permissions

**Camera & Photo Library** The camera and photo library are accessed solely to allow you to attach imagery to your plant records. Images are stored on your device and, if you enable iCloud synchronization, in your private iCloud account. They are never sent to me.

**Location Services**: The app uses location data in two ways. First, it uses GPS coordinates when you explicitly trigger the 'Get GPS Pin' function while logging an outdoor specimen; the coordinates are saved in that plant's record. Second, when you open a plant that has a saved GPS pin, the app uses your device's live location and compass heading to show where you are relative to your plants on the map and radar view. This live location is only used while that screen is open, is not saved by the app, and is never sent to me. Saved GPS pins are stored locally on your device (and in your private iCloud account if iCloud synchronization is enabled) and are not shared with any third party. Maps are shown using Apple's MapKit; if you choose to open a plant in Apple Maps, its location and name are passed to Apple Maps, and Apple's privacy policy applies.

### Your control

You stay in control of your data:

- **Delete Entries:** You can delete individual specimen logs at any time within the app.
- **Wipe Data:** Deleting the app or clearing its data via your device settings permanently removes the specimens and imagery stored on your device. If iCloud synchronization is enabled, your data remains in your private iCloud account until you delete it there.
- **Data Portability:** You can export your entire specimen database at any time as a **CSV file**. This ensures you can easily open your records in spreadsheet software (like Excel or Numbers) or move them to other applications whenever you choose.
